Set up your bot

Connect your Polymarket wallet, pick a mode and a few limits, then start. Takes about a minute.

Risk warning. This is experimental software, not financial advice. Short-term BTC markets are close to random, fees and spreads eat small edges, and you can lose everything you put in. Start in paper mode.
1
Your Polymarket wallet
Needed for live trading. Stored only in the local .env file on this computer.
Where do I find these?
  • Wallet address: on polymarket.com, open your profile menu and copy your account wallet address. This is the address that holds your Polymarket balance.
  • Private key, if you sign in with a browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, or another wallet via WalletConnect): export the key of that account from the wallet app's account details.
  • Private key, if you sign in with email or Google: Polymarket offers a key export option in your account. Follow Polymarket's own instructions.
  • Safest: create a fresh wallet just for this bot, sign in to Polymarket with it and deposit a small amount. The key controls everything in that wallet.

Starter risk limits
Conservative defaults. Everything else is in Settings.
Each trade is also capped at 2% of your bankroll and Polymarket's minimum order is 5 shares, so with the defaults you need roughly $250+ before the bot can place a trade. Change MAX_POSITION_PCT in .env only if you accept bigger bets.
Your key is saved only to .env on this computer (git-ignored, owner-only permissions). It is never shown again, logged, or sent anywhere except to sign your own Polymarket orders.
